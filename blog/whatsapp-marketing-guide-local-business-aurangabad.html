Setting Up an Irresistible WhatsApp Business Profile & Catalog

  1. Complete Business Profile: Add your exact address in Sambhajinagar, business hours, official email, and website link.
  2. Interactive Product/Service Catalog: Upload high-resolution photos of your top products or service tiers with starting prices and instant inquiry links.

Time-Saving Automation: Quick Replies, Greeting & Away Messages

  • Instant Greeting Message: Automatically welcome new clients with your consulting hours and service overview.
  • After-Hours Away Message: Set expectations when messages arrive late at night.
  • Quick Replies (/faq): Pre-saved shortcuts for common questions (e.g., typing /location sends your Google Maps address and parking directions instantly).

How to Run WhatsApp Broadcast Campaigns Without Getting Banned

To prevent WhatsApp from banning your account:

  • Only message customers who have explicitly opted in and saved your contact number.
  • Keep promotional broadcasts limited to 1–2 high-value festive offers per month.
  • Always include an easy opt-out note (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe").

Integrating WhatsApp with Your Website and Meta Ads

Connecting your digital marketing funnels directly to WhatsApp supercharges conversion:

  • Add a floating click-to-WhatsApp button on your Website.
  • Run Meta "Click-to-WhatsApp" ads targeting local shoppers in Aurangabad.

5 Essential WhatsApp Business Etiquette Rules

  1. Respect Business Hours: Only send broadcast messages between 10:00 AM and 7:30 PM to respect your customers' personal family time.
  2. Keep Messages Concise & Visual: Use bullet points, bold highlights, emojis, and high-resolution catalog images rather than long walls of dense text.
  3. Never Add Customers to Public Groups Without Consent: Always use broadcast lists or 1-on-1 chats where phone numbers remain private, protecting customer confidentiality.
  4. Respond Within 5 Minutes During Business Hours: Fast response times demonstrate professionalism and dramatically increase lead-to-sale conversion rates.
  5. Personalize Every Interaction: Always address customers by their first name and reference their previous purchase or inquiry history to build warm rapport.
