# ProVenture — 25 Local SEO Blog Prompts
**Goal:** Rank for "digital marketing / web design / graphic design in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar / Aurangabad" and related local + industry intent.

**How to use each entry:**
1. Copy the **Prompt** block into Claude/ChatGPT (or run directly in Antigravity).
2. Publish on `proventure.in/blog/` with the exact **Title** (or close variant).
3. Add the **Internal Links** mentioned — this is what actually moves local rankings, not just the content itself.
4. Add your **Google Business Profile** link + a proper `LocalBusiness`/`Article` schema block to each post.

**Standard rules for every post (don't skip):**
- Use **both** "Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar" and "Aurangabad" naturally in the H1, first 100 words, and at least one subheading — people search both, Google treats them as near-duplicates but you want to rank for both query variants.
- 1200–1800 words for pillar/service posts, 800–1200 for industry/niche posts.
- Include 1 real local reference (a landmark, area name like Cidco/Garkheda/Jalna Road/MIDC/Osmanpura, or a local business type) — generic city-name-stuffed content ranks worse than content with real local texture.
- End every post with a CTA block: WhatsApp number + "Get a free audit" link to `/contact`.
- Never invent stats, client names, or numbers you can't back up — use ranges or "based on client work" instead.

5. Group D remaining — fill in over months 2-3

## What NOT to do
- Don't publish all 25 in one week — Google treats sudden content dumps with more scrutiny; spread over 8-10 weeks (2-3/week).
- Don't reuse the same meta description/title pattern verbatim across all 25 — vary the phrasing or you risk keyword cannibalization between your own pages.
- Don't fabricate client numbers/testimonials in these posts — use real ones you have, or speak in ranges/generalities.
- Don't skip internal linking — a blog post with zero links to your service/pricing pages does nothing for lead generation, only traffic.
