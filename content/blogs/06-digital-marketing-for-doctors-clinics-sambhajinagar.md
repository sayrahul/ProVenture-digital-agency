## Optimizing Google Business Profile for Patient Appointments

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the most critical digital asset for your clinic:
- **Select Precise Primary Categories:** Use *"Orthopedic Surgeon"*, *"Pediatric Clinic"*, or *"Dental Clinic"* instead of generic *"Doctor"*.
- **Accurate OPD Hours:** Keep consulting hours updated, including separate morning and evening timings.
- **High-Resolution Facility Photography:** Showcase a clean reception, modern sterilization equipment, consultation chambers, and easy wheelchair access.
- **Direct WhatsApp Appointment Link:** Integrate a direct appointment booking link that connects prospective patients directly to your clinic reception desk.

## WhatsApp Automation: Reducing No-Shows and Follow-Up Management

Patient no-shows waste valuable OPD consulting time. Implementing simple WhatsApp automation reduces missed appointments by over 40%:
- **Instant Booking Confirmation:** Date, time slot, and Google Maps location pin.
- **Pre-Procedure Reminders:** Dietary instructions (e.g., *"Fasting required for blood tests"*).
- **Post-Care Follow-up:** Checking on patient recovery and scheduling follow-up reviews.

## What Doctors Must Strictly Avoid (Compliance & Red Flags)

- **Never Guarantee Medical Outcomes:** Avoid phrases like *"100% cure guaranteed"* or *"painless surgery guaranteed"*.
- **Protect Patient Privacy:** Never post identifiable patient photographs, X-rays, or case histories without written, signed consent.
- **No Disparaging Competitors:** Ethical marketing highlights your qualifications and patient care without criticizing other medical practitioners.
