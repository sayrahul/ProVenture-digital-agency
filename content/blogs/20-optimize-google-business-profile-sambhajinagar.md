## The Anatomy of the Google Local 3-Pack Algorithm

Google determines local ranking based on three core pillars:
1. **Relevance:** How closely your profile matches what the user is searching for.
2. **Distance / Proximity:** How close your verified business location is to the searcher in Aurangabad.
3. **Prominence:** How established, active, and well-reviewed your business is across the web.

---

## Step 1: Selecting the Exact Primary & Secondary Categories

Your primary category carries the heaviest algorithmic weight:
- Be specific: Choose *"Orthopedic Clinic"* instead of generic *"Doctor"*, or *"Italian Restaurant"* instead of generic *"Restaurant"*.
- Add up to 5 relevant secondary categories (e.g., *Sports Medicine Clinic, Physical Therapy Center*).

---

## Step 2: Nailing Your NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone)

- **Name:** Use your exact registered legal business name. Do not stuff spammy keywords (e.g., writing *"ProVenture Best Digital Marketing Agency Cheap"* will trigger an instant Google suspension).
- **Address:** Provide exact street details, landmark (e.g., *"Opposite Prozone Mall, CIDCO"*), and accurate 431001/431003 pincodes.
- **Phone:** Use a direct local mobile or landline number with instant response capability.
