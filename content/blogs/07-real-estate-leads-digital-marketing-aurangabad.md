## Hypertargeted Meta Lead Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Meta advertising is the most powerful volume lead driver for residential real estate in Aurangabad when configured correctly:

### 1. Audience Segmentation
- **First-Time Home Buyers (Age 27–38):** Target young IT professionals, MIDC engineers, and newly married couples searching for affordable 1 & 2 BHK flats (₹25L – ₹45L).
- **Luxury & Upgrade Buyers (Age 38–58):** Target established business owners, doctors, and industrial executives in Osmanpura and Samarth Nagar looking for premium 3 & 4 BHK row houses and penthouses (₹75L – ₹1.5Cr+).
- **Investors (Sambhajinagar, Pune, Mumbai, NRI):** Highlight rental yield and commercial capital appreciation near AURIC Shendra.

### 2. High-Converting Ad Creative Angles
- Use real video walkthroughs of the sample flat instead of 3D architectural renders.
- Clearly mention the starting price and RERA registration number in the ad copy to filter out unqualified low-budget inquiries.
