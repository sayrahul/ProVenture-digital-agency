## The Platform Risk: Why Rented Land Threatens Your Business

When your entire revenue pipeline depends exclusively on Instagram and WhatsApp:
1. **Algorithm Changes Can Wipe Out Reach Overnight:** Meta regularly updates its algorithms. A business that received 50 daily inquiries can suddenly drop to 5 inquiries after a single algorithm tweak.
2. **Accidental Account Suspensions:** Automated copyright flags or competitor spam reports can lead to sudden account lockouts with zero human customer support to help you appeal.
3. **Zero Data Ownership:** You don't own your followers' contact information. If your account is lost, your entire customer list vanishes.
